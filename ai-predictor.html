The Problem

Students entering the workforce need clearer guidance on AI's impact.

Many students are entering a rapidly changing workforce without a clear understanding of how artificial intelligence may reshape different careers and industries. Most conversations around AI and jobs are either overly optimistic or fear-driven, making it difficult for students to find practical, accessible information about workforce trends, automation risk, and future-ready skills.

I wanted to build a platform that could make these ideas easier to explore interactively and help students think more critically about the future of work, technology, and career planning.

What I Built

An interactive platform for exploring AI's impact on careers.

I developed the AI Job Impact Predictor, a web-based application that analyzes how emerging AI technologies may affect different professions and industries. The platform allows users to explore projected automation exposure, industry trends, and skill relevance through interactive visualizations and career insights.

The application was built using Python, Streamlit, data analysis libraries, and publicly available workforce and technology trend data. I focused on creating an interface that made complex labor and AI-related information understandable and engaging for students and non-technical users.

🔍 Risk Classification
Low / Medium / High risk scores for 700+ occupations with probability breakdowns.
💡 AI Explainability
Explains the specific factors driving each job's automation risk — not just a score.
⚖️ Career Comparison
Side-by-side risk comparison of any two occupations to support career decisions.
🛡️ Skill Recommendations
Identifies which skills reduce automation risk for any selected role.
📊 Data Visualizations
Risk by job category and automation risk vs. employment scatter plot.

Safest and riskiest jobs at a glance for quick reference and exploration.
