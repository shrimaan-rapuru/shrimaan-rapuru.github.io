About

I'm a junior at The Early College at Guilford — a dual-enrollment program where I take college-level coursework at Guilford College alongside high school — carrying a 4.0 GPA and 8 AP scores of 5.

My work sits at the intersection of hardware and software. I've built deployed web apps, led robotics hardware systems, and currently research synthetic bacteriophage genome engineering at NC A&T using PCR, Gel Electrophoresis, and Gibson Assembly.

Outside the lab, I coordinate weekly meals for 75–100 homeless residents at Open Door Ministries — funding the program myself through a part-time job — and co-manage a 7-unit residential property portfolio.

I'm drawn to engineering problems where the technical solution has to actually fit the people it serves. That gap — between something that works in theory and something that works in the world — is where I want to spend my career.
